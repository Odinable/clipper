Tutorial

From stream to reels in five minutes.

Clipper watches your stream, catches the hype moments and cuts them into vertical reels while you play. Here's everything you need to know to use it.

Getting startedLive modeClipping by hand Your reelsAfter the streamSettingsTipsTroubleshooting
01 · Getting started

Open Clipper.

  1. Open ClipperFrom the Start menu or desktop shortcut. The Clipper window opens by itself.
  2. Sign in onceThe first time, click Continue and enter your name and email, and you're straight back in Clipper. It's free, and you only do it once on each PC.
  3. Pick a tabLive is for while you're streaming. After stream is for a finished stream or a recording on your PC.
  4. Close it when you're doneClosing the window quits Clipper. If a reel is still being made, it finishes first.
Clipper runs on your PC only. Your video isn't uploaded anywhere. See the privacy policy.
02 · Live mode

Reels while you stream.

  1. Go live on YouTube firstStart your stream as normal in OBS.
  2. Copy your live linkOpen your stream on YouTube and copy the address from the top of the browser, like https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=…. In YouTube Studio you can also use Share on the live dashboard.
  3. Paste it into “Your live stream link”Clipper remembers it, so next time it may already be filled in.
  4. Choose a reel style, length and how pickySee Settings below. The defaults (Full game, 30s, balanced) are a good start.
  5. Hit Start watchingIt shows CONNECTING, then LEARNING for about 90 seconds while it gets used to your normal volume, then WATCHING.

What you'll see while live

  • The clock: how long Clipper has been watching.
  • Hype right now: a live graph of how hyped the stream is. When the line crosses the dashed clip line, a reel is made.
  • Reels made / being cut / chat: “chat on” means it's reading your live chat too.

What counts as a hype moment

  • Sound: much louder than the last few minutes, like you shouting or laughing, crashes, gunfire or explosions.
  • Chat: lots of messages at once, words like “LMAO”, “no way” or 💀, and Super Chats.

Each reel takes the build-up before the moment and your reaction after it. It's ready about 10 seconds after the clip ends. YouTube's own delay adds 5–20 seconds on top.

03 · Clipping by hand

Saw something good? Clip it.

Anywhere on your PC

Press F9

Works even when OBS or another window is in front. Best while you're playing.

In the Clipper window

CLIP THAT

The big orange button. The space bar works too while the window is open.

From chat

!clip

Anyone typing !clip in your chat clips the moment, so your mods can clip for you.

A hand clip grabs the last ~25 seconds plus 5 seconds after, because you press it right after the moment happens.

04 · Your reels

Check, caption, post.

Reels appear on the right as they're made: first “Moment caught”, then “Cutting reel…”, then the finished video. The newest is first, with an orange outline.

  • Play it right in the card.
  • Add a caption: type in the box under the reel and press Enter. The reel is re-made with a big title at the top.
  • Show file opens the folder with the reel selected, ready to drag into YouTube Shorts, TikTok or Instagram.
  • 16:9 opens the normal widescreen version (if you ticked “Also make a normal 16:9 clip”).
  • Delete hides a reel you don't want. The file stays in the folder.
  • Copy timestamps copies the time of every highlight, ready to paste into your VOD description.
  • The menu at the top switches between past streams. Open folder shows that stream's files.
05 · After the stream

Every highlight, cut.

  1. Open the After stream tab
  2. Paste your stream's link or click Browse…A YouTube link downloads the stream and its chat replay first (long streams take a while). Browse… picks a recording on your PC, like your OBS recording.
  3. Choose how many clips5, 10, 15 or 25. It picks the strongest moments across the whole stream.
  4. Hit Find highlightsThe bar shows what it's doing. When it says Done, your clips are on the right, with a timeline showing where each one came from.
06 · Settings

Pick your look.

Reel style

Full gameThe whole picture in the middle over a blurred copy. Nothing gets cut off.
Cam + gameYour webcam on top, the game below. The classic streamer Short.
ZoomedThe middle of the game fills the whole screen. The edges are cut off.
When you pick Cam + game, tell Clipper which corner your webcam is in. It expects a webcam about a quarter of the screen wide (480 × 270 on a 1080p stream), near the edge. If your cam is bigger or somewhere unusual, use Full game.
SettingWhat it does
Reel length20, 30, 45 or 60 seconds. 30 is the sweet spot for Shorts and TikTok.
How pickyLeft = more reels, including smaller moments. Right = only the big ones. Start in the middle and adjust after one stream.
Tag on reelsA small orange label above your caption, like “GTA VI · LIVE” or your channel name. Leave it empty for none.
Caption on every reelOptional title on every reel from this session. You can also caption reels one by one later.
Also make a normal 16:9 clipMakes a widescreen copy as well, for YouTube or X.
07 · Tips

Get better reels.

  • React out loud. Clipper hears you. Big reactions make the best reels anyway.
  • Use F9 for quiet moments. A clean stunt or a funny moment with no noise won't be caught on its own.
  • Turn the car radio down in game if it plays the whole time. Constant music hides the action.
  • Too many reels? Move “How picky” right. Too few? Move it left.
  • Only clip your own streams, or streams you have permission to clip.
08 · Troubleshooting

Something not working?

It stays on CONNECTING.
Check the stream is actually live and set to Public or Unlisted (Private streams can't be read). Open Details at the bottom left for the exact reason. Clipper keeps retrying by itself.
No reels are appearing.
For the first 90 seconds it's learning your volume, so only F9 and CLIP THAT work. After that, move “How picky” to the left, or clip by hand.
F9 does nothing.
Another app may already be using F9. The top right of the Clipper window says “F9 unavailable” if so. Use the CLIP THAT button or !clip in chat.
Where are my reels saved?
In the out folder inside your Clipper folder, one folder per stream. Click Open folder in the app.
The window says “Clipper app closed”.
Open Clipper again. Your reels are safe in the folder.

Still stuck? Contact us. Click Copy app details on that page and paste it into your email so we can see what happened.

Privacy policy

Your streams stay on your PC.

Clipper is a desktop app made by Growthforge. It runs on your own computer, and nothing you clip is sent to us. The only thing we receive is the name and email you sign in with. This page explains what it does touch, and why.

Effective 20 November 2026 · Applies to the Clipper desktop app only. Our website has its own privacy policy.

In short

The short version.

(01)

No uploads

Your video, reels and chat are processed on your computer. Growthforge never receives them.

(02)

Just an email sign-in

You sign in once with your email. No analytics, no ads, no tracking of what you clip or when.

(03)

You're in control

Everything Clipper saves is in its own folder. Delete the folder and it's gone.

Chat messages include the names and words of people in your chat. Clipper only counts and scores them to find hype moments. It doesn't build profiles or send them anywhere.

Other services

Who else is involved.

YouTube. To read a stream, Clipper connects to YouTube the way a viewer would, using the open-source tool yt-dlp. YouTube sees that connection from your computer, as it would for any viewer, and Google's privacy policy applies to it. Clipper doesn't sign in to your YouTube account. If you paste a link from another site, the same applies to that site.

Links you click. Links to our website, email or booking page open in your normal web browser or mail app, and those sites' own policies apply.

Growthforge sign-in. The sign-in page is on growthforge.cloud. Sign-ups are stored in Google Sheets. It's covered by our website's privacy policy, which also explains how long we keep sign-ups and your rights.

Apart from your stream's site and the sign-in page, Clipper makes no other internet connections. Its fonts and images are built in.

Your choices

Deleting your data.

  • One reel: delete its files from the session folder (Show file takes you there). The Delete button only hides a reel in the app.
  • One stream: delete its folder inside the out folder in your Clipper folder (Open folder takes you there).
  • Everything on your PC: delete the out and vods folders, .appwindow for saved settings and .account.json for your sign-in. Or uninstall Clipper and delete its folder.
  • Sign out: click Sign out at the bottom left of the app. This removes the access pass from your PC.
  • Your email on our list: email us and we'll delete it. Every marketing email also has an unsubscribe link.

We never receive your streams, chat or reels, so there's nothing of those to delete on our side.

Your part

Using it fairly.

Only clip streams you own or have permission to clip, and follow YouTube's terms and the rules of any platform you post reels to. You're responsible for the reels you publish.
